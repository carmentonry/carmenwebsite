About

Hi, I'm Carmen. I do a bit of everything in travel and media, and I'm always chasing new places and new experiences.

My work connects journalism, travel, and digital media: I host international group trips, help travelers plan their own adventures, and tell stories through writing, photo, and video. Studying, traveling, and living abroad showed me how much there is to learn from people and cultures different from my own, and now I want to help others build a life of travel and freedom, too.
